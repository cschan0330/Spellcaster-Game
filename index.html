05 A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you
turn the key.

A few answers for the road ahead.

✳
What is My Summer Car?+

A car-building and life-survival simulation set in the Finnish countryside of the 1990s. You assemble and maintain a car while also looking after yourself.

I am new. Where should I begin?+

Start with the six-step beginner checklist. Get familiar with the controls and garage, plan supplies, then tackle the car one system at a time.

How do I use this wiki?+

Search the topic directory or choose a category. Open any guide for a short reference, and use the build overview to organize your next workshop session.

Is this an official website?+

No. This is an independent fan reference. My Summer Car and its game imagery belong to Amistech Games.

Where can I find official information?+

Use the official game website and Steam store links in the footer for the game description, announcements, and purchase information.

Does this replace a complete assembly manual?+

No. These are concise planning and maintenance references. Exact fasteners, adjustments, and part-by-part installation require a dedicated, version-appropriate manual.
